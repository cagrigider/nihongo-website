Delete your NihonGo account

This page explains how to delete your account in NihonGo, the Japanese learning app for Android and iOS by Cagri Gider, and which data is deleted.

Option 1: delete your account in the app

  1. Open NihonGo and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account, then Permanently Delete Account.
  3. Confirm, then confirm it's you with the Google account you signed in with.

Your account and the data listed under What is deleted are deleted right away, while you wait on the deletion screen. If the deletion cannot finish (for example because you are offline), the app tells you so and nothing is reported as deleted until it has been; if your sign-in account was removed but the data on your device was not, the app removes that data the next time it starts.

Option 2: request deletion without the app

If you can no longer use the app, send an email to cagri.gider.business@gmail.com with the subject Account deletion request, sent from the email address of the Google account you signed in to NihonGo with. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address before deleting anything, so that nobody else can delete your account.

Requests by email are handled by hand. We confirm that we received your request within 7 days, and delete your account and the online data listed below within 30 days of receiving it (or of your confirmation, if we asked for one). We email you when it is done. Data stored on your device is not reachable by us: uninstall the app to remove it.

What is deleted

  • Your NihonGo sign-in account.
  • Your learning progress stored online (vocabulary, kanji, study time and activity history).
  • Your notification registration record.
  • Your referral code and its records, and any referral you redeemed, if you used the referral feature.
  • When you delete in the app: all NihonGo data stored on that device for your account.

What is not deleted

  • Subscriptions are not cancelled. Purchases are handled by Google Play, not by NihonGo. Cancel your subscription there before deleting your account to stop future charges: Google Play subscriptions. Google keeps its own purchase records under its own policies.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports. The app sends app-usage events (for example that a learning session was completed, or that the subscription screen was shown) to Google Analytics for Firebase, and crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics. They are never linked to your NihonGo account: no account identifier, name, email address or learning content is included. Because they cannot be traced back to your account they are not deleted with it; Google keeps them for the retention periods of those Firebase services, and we use them only to understand usage in aggregate and to fix crashes.
  • Device identifiers used by Firebase. Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications) and Firebase Installations use an identifier of the app installation on your device, not of your account. It is not deleted with your account; it stops being used when you uninstall the app, and expires after a period of inactivity.
  • If another user redeemed your referral code, their own record of that redemption belongs to their account and is removed when they delete theirs.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. If you only want to stop using NihonGo on one device, uninstalling the app removes the data stored on that device.
