Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with NihonGo?
Download the app, create an account, and choose your Japanese learning level. Start with kana practice or jump into AI conversations to begin your learning journey.
What Japanese levels does NihonGo support?
NihonGo supports all levels from complete beginner (N5) to advanced (N1), with content automatically adapting to your current proficiency.
How does the AI conversation feature work?
Our AI creates realistic Japanese conversations based on your level. Practice common scenarios like ordering food, asking directions, or casual conversation with instant feedback.
Can I use NihonGo offline?
Yes! Vocabulary practice and kana review work offline. AI conversations require an internet connection for real-time processing.
How do I track my progress?
Your progress is automatically tracked across all features. View detailed analytics, learning streaks, and achievements in the app dashboard.
Is my learning data private?
Absolutely. Your data is encrypted and never shared with third parties. We use it only to personalize your learning experience.
How do I reset my progress?
Go to Settings > Reset Progress. You can reset individual areas or start completely fresh with a new account.
What devices are supported?
NihonGo works on iPhone and iPad running iOS 14.0 or later. Your progress syncs across all your devices.
How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?
Use the contact form below or email us directly. We read every message and respond within 24 hours.
